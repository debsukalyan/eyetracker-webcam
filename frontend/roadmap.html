STOP 2

Pick the right exam

Exams decide which door you use, not where you end up. Most people need only one or two of these.

STOP 3

Get into your first lab

Every route needs the same three things: one proof project, some real lab exposure, and one good email to the right lab.

Lab exposure you can apply for

IASc–INSA–NASI Summer Fellowship

Eight weeks in a research lab for UG and PG students across India.

Applications usually open around the start of the year.

TIFR VSRP

TIFR's Visiting Students' Research Programme, a summer project in a TIFR lab.

Good for biology, physics and maths students.

Institute summer internships

Most IISc, IISER, NCBS and NBRC labs take summer interns when you email the PI directly.

Use the email template below.

Neuromatch Academy

Online computational neuroscience school with group projects. Free course material at neuromatch.io.

The fastest bridge for engineers and physicists.

Build one proof project (no lab needed)

EEG

Load the sample data in MNE-Python, find the brain's response to a sound or image, and plot it.

fMRI

Pick a dataset on OpenNeuro and reproduce one figure from its paper.

Neurons

Use the Allen Brain Map data to compare how neurons in two brain areas fire.

Behaviour

Run a reaction-time or memory experiment on 20 friends and analyse it in Python or R.

Put it on GitHub with a one-page write-up. One finished project beats five certificates.

The email to a lab

Short, specific, and proof that you read their work. Replace the orange parts.

Subject: [Your degree] student interested in [their topic] in your lab

Dear Prof. [Name],

I am a [year, degree] student at [college]. I read your paper on [topic, year], and the part about [one specific finding] made me want to learn how you [method they used].

I recently [your proof project, one line] ([GitHub link]). I would like to work in your lab as a [summer intern / project student] from [month] to [month].

I have attached my CV. Thank you for your time.

[Your name]
  • Under 150 words
  • One paper, one specific detail
  • One link to your project
  • No reply in 10 days? Follow up once
STOP 4

MSc, Integrated PhD or PhD

After BSc you can go straight into an Integrated PhD. After MSc, BE or BTech you apply for a PhD. Most PhDs in India come with a monthly fellowship.

Where neuroscience happens in India

A starting list, not a ranking. Read the faculty pages and shortlist labs, not just institutes.

IISc, Bengaluru

Centre for Neuroscience: cognitive, systems, computational, molecular.

NBRC, Manesar

National Brain Research Centre, India's dedicated brain research institute.

NCBS, Bengaluru

Neurobiology, behaviour, circuits.

TIFR, Mumbai

Neuroscience within its biological sciences.

IISERs

Pune and others have neuroscience and behaviour labs.

Cognitive Science

IIT Kanpur, IIIT Hyderabad and CBCS Allahabad.

NIMHANS, Bengaluru

Clinical neuroscience, neurophysiology, psychiatry research.

STOP 5

Where a neuroscientist works

"Will I get a job?" is the second most common question in my comments. A PhD is not the only exit, and academia is not the only job.

Academia

Postdoc, then faculty. Run your own lab, teach, publish.

Neurotech & BCI

EEG headsets, brain-computer interfaces, neurostimulation startups.

Data science & AI

Neural-data skills (signals, stats, ML) transfer straight to tech.

Pharma & clinical research

Drug discovery for brain disorders, clinical trials, medical affairs.

Consumer & UX research

Neuromarketing, behavioural science and product research teams.

Science communication & policy

Writing, content, science journalism, research funding and policy.

Clinical neuropsychology

Assessing and treating patients. In India this needs RCI-recognised clinical training.
