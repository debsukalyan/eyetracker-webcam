The 10-minute challenge.

Mini consumer research challenge

Imagine a new chewable product for everyday energy and focus.

It is meant for students and young professionals who currently rely on coffee, energy drinks or similar products.

  1. Spend about 10 minutes looking at this space online.
  2. Tell us 3 things products in this space keep saying.
  3. Tell us 1 thing that feels repetitive or boring.
  4. Tell us 1 thing you would want to learn from real consumers before launch.
  5. In 3 simple steps, tell us how you would test that question.

No jargon needed. We are looking for observation, curiosity and whether your test makes sense.
